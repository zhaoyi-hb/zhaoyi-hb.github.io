Yi Zhao 赵祎

Ph.D. Candidate · Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG), Tsinghua University

I am a Ph.D. candidate and a member of the Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG), Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, where I am fortunate to work with Prof. Jie Tang. I am advised by Prof. Jibing Gong at Yanshan University. My research interests lie in recommendation systems, cross-domain recommendation, knowledge graphs, and graph neural networks, together with their intersection with large language and vision–language models. Currently, I focus on transferring knowledge across domains and building recommender systems that reason over structured knowledge with the help of large models. If these directions align with your interests, feel free to reach out — I am always happy to discuss ideas and explore collaborations.
